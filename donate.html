Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I donate from outside India (NRI / Overseas)?

Yes! International devotees can send contributions via wire transfer or through authorized Indian bank accounts. Please write to donate@kamalpurabhijaan.org for swift international wiring instructions.

How do I receive my Puja Pushpanjali and Bhog token?

When you donate online, you will receive an automated receipt and a unique Token QR Code on your WhatsApp number, which can be presented at the Sangha counter for consecrated Bhog packages.

Can I sponsor a specific community welfare initiative?

Absolutely. You can designate your funds exclusively for Blood Donation camps, the Youth Education & AI Academy, or Winter Blanket distributions by selecting the fund category on the donation form.
