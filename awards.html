Prestigious Recognitions · রাষ্ট্রীয় ও সামাজিক সম্মান

Celebrated Accolades Across Bengal & India

“শিল্প, ঐতিহ্য, শৃঙ্খলা ও সেবার মেলবন্ধনে অর্জিত স্বীকৃতি”

Over five decades of benchmark execution has won Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha highest-tier government honors, administrative accolades, and cultural commendations.

🏆
Government of West Bengal

Biswa Bangla Sharad Samman (বিশ্ব বাংলা শারদ সম্মান)

Conferred for Best Thematic Pandal Architecture & Idol Craftsmanship. Honoring our preservation of Bengal's traditional clay arts, Kumartuli and Krishnanagar master sculpture, and spiritual authenticity.

🛡️
Nadia District Police & Administration

Exemplary Crowd Safety & Fire Protection Award

Recognized for setting the benchmark in pilgrim safety, disaster mitigation, CCTV command centers, separated emergency corridors, and managing over 2.5 million visitors with zero untoward incidents.

🌿
WB Pollution Control & Green Directorate

West Bengal Green Earth & Eco-Puja Honor

Awarded for our strict Zero-Plastic Policy, 100% natural biodegradable construction (bamboo, jute, hay, clay), organic water-soluble vegetable colors, and dedicated waste recycling units.

🩸
Indian Red Cross Society

Highest Voluntary Blood Collection Citation

Commended as the premier youth organization in Nadia district, collecting over 300+ units of voluntary life-saving blood annually during our Maha Raktadan Shivir.

✨
Bengal Lighting Artisans Guild

All-Bengal Illumination Excellence Trophy

Celebrated for our landmark 1.5 km Chandannagar light boulevard along Ranaghat Station Road and kinetic 3D mythological LED narrative arches engineered with solar-hybrid energy.

🏛️
Nadia Zilla Parishad

Nadia Architectural Heritage Gold Trophy

Bestowed in recognition of our grand multi-pavilion concept, masterfully recreating historical Bengal terracotta temples, Bishnupur motifs, and authentic heritage sanctums.

55+ Years of Dedicated Seva

A Legacy of Faith, Artistry & Social Transformation

From humble beginnings in 1972 when neighborhood youth gathered under sacred banyan trees to worship Maa Durga and uplift the community, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha has blossomed into Nadia's crown jewel of socio-cultural devotion.

117
Divine Forms of Durga presented across 80 temple pavilions in 2026.
2.5M+
Devotees & Pilgrims welcomed annually with safety and warm hospitality.
25,000+
Maha Bhog Packets distributed daily during festive days free of cost.
300+
Units of Blood donated annually through continuous youth health camps.
