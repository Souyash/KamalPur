Leadership From the President's Desk

A Message from
Our President

Reflections and vision from Sri Subir Roy Chowdhury, President of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha, on conducting Ranaghat's landmark Durga Puja and empowering our community through seva.

Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha
Subir Roy Chowdhury
President · Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha · 2024–2026
Presidential Address

Rooted in devotion,
driven by selfless service

“A festival is not measured solely by the grandeur of its pavilions, but by the love and seva it pours into people's hearts. At Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha, every edition of Durga Puja is conducted as an offering to Maa and the people of Ranaghat.”

Dear devotees, members, pilgrims, and well-wishers across Bengal and the world,

It is with profound humility and gratitude that I address you on behalf of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha. As we celebrate over 55 unbroken years of existence, I remember with deep reverence our visionary founding elders who in 1970 established our neighborhood Durga Puja and youth sports society.

Today, our annual Durgotsav has become a national phenomenon. With our 2026 milestone presentation of the historic 117 Divine Forms of Maa Durga across 80 temple pavilions, 1.5 km of Chandannagar light boulevards, and daily Maha Bhog for 25,000+ devotees, we are honored to conduct Ranaghat's premier celebration.

Yet our social commitment remains our greatest duty: every volunteer hour and contribution fuels our blood donation camps, free health and eye clinics near Kamalpur Primary Health Centre, and football academy for youth.

Subir Roy Chowdhury
President, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha
Future Roadmap

Vision 2026–2030: Our Commitments

The executive committee under the President’s guidance has formulated four landmark commitments for our upcoming golden milestone decade:

Grassroots Sports Academy

Providing professional free football and athletic coaching, gear, and nutrition to 150 local youngsters.

Expanded Seva Health Center

Upgrading the Sangha medical dispensary to provide daily subsidized pathology tests and dialysis aid.

100% Eco-Friendly Pandal Norms

Zero-plastic festival mandate, organic clay and natural colors, with solar energy powered festival gates.

Digital Heritage Archive

Digitizing over 50 years of festival publications, photographs, and Nadia folk art oral histories.
