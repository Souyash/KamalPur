Leadership From the President's Desk

A Message from
Our President

Thoughts, reflections, and future roadmap from Sri Subir Roy Chowdhury, President of Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha.

Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha
Subir Roy Chowdhury
President · Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha · 2024–2026
Presidential Address

Rooted in friendship,
driven by selfless service

“A club is not measured by the height of its pandal walls, but by the depth of its roots in the hearts of its neighbors. At Kamalpur, every achievement is dedicated to the people of Ranaghat.”

Dear members, devotees, and well-wishers across Bengal and the world,

It is with profound humility and gratitude that I address you on behalf of Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha. As we stand proudly beyond 55 unbroken years of existence, I look back with immense reverence to the brave, visionary founding members who in 1970 laid the cornerstone of our institution.

Today, our annual Durgotsav has become a national phenomenon. Whether through monumental thematic installations like the acclaimed 117 Divine Forms of Durga or our magnificent illumination boulevards, we strive to elevate Bengal’s artistic standard. Yet, I assure every resident that our social responsibility remains paramount: every rupee and volunteer hour is equally committed to our voluntary blood donation camps, free medical diagnostics, and youth football programs.

I warmly invite our young generation and the global Bengali diaspora to connect with our Sangha. Let us continue to build an organization where empathy, culture, and sports thrive hand in hand.

Subir Roy Chowdhury
President, Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha
Future Roadmap

Vision 2026–2030: Our Commitments

The executive committee under the President’s guidance has formulated four landmark commitments for our upcoming golden milestone decade:

⚽
Grassroots Sports Academy

Providing professional free football and athletic coaching, gear, and nutrition to 150 local youngsters.

🩺
Expanded Seva Health Center

Upgrading the Sangha medical dispensary to provide daily subsidized pathology tests and dialysis aid.

🌿
100% Eco-Friendly Pandal Norms

Zero-plastic festival mandate, organic clay and natural colors, with solar energy powered festival gates.

💻
Digital Heritage Archive

Digitizing over 50 years of festival publications, photographs, and Nadia folk art oral histories.

Architect of the first youth constitution and playground acquisition.

Late Anil Baran Ghosh
President (1977–1985)

Inaugurated the Annual Blood Donation Drive and permanent clubhouse.

Sri Bhabesh Chandra Roy
President (1986–1998)

Expanded the Kamalpur Gold Cup Football League across Bengal.

Sri Santosh Kumar Banerjee
President (1999–2012)

Pioneered large-scale architectural theme Durga Pujas in Nadia district.
