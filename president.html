#1 · President
1. Ashim Biswas
President · Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha · 2024–2026
01 · Presidential Address

1. Rooted in devotion,
driven by selfless service

“A festival is not measured solely by the grandeur of its pavilions, but by the love and seva it pours into people's hearts. At Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha, every edition of Durga Puja is conducted as an offering to Maa and the people of Ranaghat.”

Dear devotees, members, pilgrims, and well-wishers across Bengal and the world,

It is with profound humility and gratitude that I address you on behalf of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha. As we celebrate over 55 unbroken years of existence, I remember with deep reverence our visionary founding elders who in 1972 established our neighborhood Durga Puja and community welfare society.

Today, our annual Durgotsav has become a national phenomenon. With our 2026 milestone presentation of the historic 117 Divine Forms of Maa Durga across 80 temple pavilions, 1.5 km of Chandannagar light boulevards, and daily Maha Bhog for 25,000+ devotees, we are honored to conduct Ranaghat's premier celebration.

Yet our social commitment remains our greatest duty: every volunteer hour and contribution fuels our blood donation camps, free health and eye clinics near Kamalpur Primary Health Centre, and education & AI coaching labs for youth.

1. Ashim Biswas
President, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha (2024–2026)

2. Fostering fellowship &
community harmony

“Our strength lies in the unity of our residents, our youth volunteers, and our collective dedication to Maa Durga and community welfare across Ranaghat.”

As Vice-President of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha, it is a privilege to support our executive leadership in mobilizing volunteers, patron outreach, and civic integration across Nadia.

Every edition of our Durgotsav brings together families from all walks of life. From coordinating community health checkups and blood donation drives to fostering educational coaching and job hubs, our commitment is to keep the sacred flame of service alive for generations to come.

03 · Chief Guest & Coordinator's Address

3. Uniting faith, governance &
universal welfare

“শারদোৎসব শুধু আনন্দের মিলনমেলা নয়, মানুষের পাশে দাঁড়িয়ে সেবার মহোৎসব। কামালপুর অভিযান সংঘের রক্তদান শিবির, বস্ত্র বিতরণ ও খাদ্য সেবায় নিবেদিত প্রাণ সকলের জন্য এক উজ্জ্বল দৃষ্টান্ত।”

As Chief Guest and Coordinator, it gives me profound joy to support Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha's monumental celebration.

The Sangha’s visionary portrayal of the 117 Divine Forms of Maa Durga across 80 temple pavilions elevates the cultural prestige of Ranaghat and Bengal across India. Alongside this grand artistic pilgrimage, their uninterrupted humanitarian work — free healthcare camps, blood donation drives, educational aid, and community nourishment — represents the true spirit of Durga Puja.

3. Ashok Krittonia

4. Sujay Biswas
General Secretary · Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha · 2024–2026
04 · General Secretary's Address

4. Executing our vision with
dedication & harmony

“Organizing Ranaghat's landmark celebration across 80 temple pavilions demands precision, public safety, and profound devotion. Our youth volunteers and administrative teams work day and night to ensure every pilgrim experiences Maa's divine grace seamlessly.”

Dear respected patrons, festival partners, members, and devotees,

It is my proud privilege as General Secretary to welcome you to Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha's historic 2026 Sharodotsav. Turning the grand vision of our President Sri Ashim Biswas and Executive Committee into reality is a sacred endeavor shared by hundreds of dedicated young volunteers across Nadia.

This year, we have coordinated with the Ranaghat Municipality, Nadia District Police, fire and medical services, and over 120 Krishnanagar & Kumartuli master artisans. Together, we are creating a world-class pilgrimage infrastructure with zero-plastic eco-friendly materials, round-the-clock medical camps, and organized queues for over 2.5 million expected footfalls.

Beyond the festive days, our office coordinates round-the-year youth education & AI coaching, digital literacy, and community emergency relief. We thank you for your unwavering trust and support.

4. Sujay Biswas
General Secretary, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha (2024–2026)

Future Roadmap

Vision 2026–2030: Our Commitments

The executive committee under the President’s guidance has formulated four landmark commitments for our upcoming golden milestone decade:

Education & AI Academy

Providing free Spoken English, Generative AI skills, competitive exam coaching, and career guidance to 150+ local youngsters.

Expanded Seva Health Center

Upgrading the Sangha medical dispensary to provide daily subsidized pathology tests and dialysis aid.

100% Eco-Friendly Pandal Norms

Zero-plastic festival mandate, organic clay and natural colors, with solar energy powered festival gates.

Digital Heritage Archive

Digitizing over 50 years of festival publications, photographs, and Nadia folk art oral histories.
