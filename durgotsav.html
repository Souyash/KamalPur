Ranaghat Sharodotsav —
Conducted by Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha

Welcoming millions of pilgrims and art connoisseurs to Bengal's benchmark Durgotsav celebration. Home to the sensational 117 Divine Forms of Maa Durga housed across 80 temple pavilions, illuminated boulevards, and sacred Vedic traditions.

2026 Iconic Presentation

117 Forms of Maa Durga in 80 Mandir Pavilions

Conducted by Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha (কামালপুর অভিযান সংঘ), Ranaghat's Durgotsav is known across India for audacious sculptural artistry and cultural devotion. In 2026, the Sangha proudly presents its most spiritually ambitious and visually staggering masterpiece: 117 distinct iconography forms of Goddess Durga housed within 80 sanctum pavilions.

Sculpted by master clay artisans of Krishnanagar and Kumartuli, each form portrays a divine cosmic aspect documented in Vedic texts and Bengal's Shakta tradition — from Mahamaya, Mahishasuramardini, and Navadurga to revered regional guardian deities. Devotees walk through a continuous sacred complex constructed with eco-friendly bamboo, jute, and terracotta.

Visitor Assistance

How to Visit Kamalpur Durgotsav

By Train / Local Transit

Board any Sealdah-Ranaghat / Shantipur / Gede local train from Sealdah Station (approx 1 hr 45 min).

From Ranaghat Junction (Platform 1 Exit) or Habibpur Railway Station, take an auto-rickshaw or e-rickshaw (toto) directly to Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha Ground (Near Kamalpur Primary Health Centre). Dedicated festival totos operate 24 hours throughout all five puja days.

By Road / Highway (NH-12)

Drive along National Highway 12 (formerly NH-34) towards Ranaghat, Nadia. Enter via the Ranaghat College bypass leading towards Kamalpur Road.

Designated Free Parking: Ranaghat Town Club Ground and Kamalpur School Grounds (monitored with CCTV surveillance, volunteer guides, and clear signage).
