Festivals, Sports &
Seva Conducted by the Sangha

At Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha, every season brings a new celebration of devotion, athletic energy, or social giving — anchored by Ranaghat's grandest Durgotsav. Explore our complete annual schedule.

Oct16–20
Flagship Festival · 2026
Cultural & Devotional
Ranaghat Sharodotsav: Durgotsav 2026

Conducted by Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha · Directed by Event Manager Subha Kumar Mandal (SUBHA-ARTHA). Featuring the historic 117 forms of Goddess Durga across 80 temple pavilions, Chandannagar lights, Maha Bhog seva, and nightly concerts.
