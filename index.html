— WHO WE ARE

Not just a club —
a family

Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha was born in 1972 in Kamalpur, Ranaghat, Nadia out of a passionate collective dream: to unite our community through the sacred celebration of Maa Durga and to serve the people through selfless humanitarian works.

Over the past 55+ years, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha has established Ranaghat on the global map of Durga Puja. Devotees and visitors travel from across West Bengal, Kolkata, and beyond to witness our pathbreaking festival creations — from the record-setting 112-foot idol to our 2026 spiritual landmark of 117 divine forms of Goddess Durga across 80 temple pavilions.

This same festival spirit fuels our year-round social welfare: conducting Nadia district's largest annual blood donation drives, running free health and eye clinics near Kamalpur Primary Health Centre, fostering youth career skills through Spoken English and AI coaching classes, and distributing daily Maha Bhog to over 25,000 pilgrims.

Culture & Arts
Pioneering architectural pandal arts, Rabindra-Nazrul Jayanti, drama, baul sangeet, and youth recitations.
Community Welfare
Bi-annual blood donation drives, Bostro & Kombol Bitoron, free medical diagnostics, and disaster relief.
Education & Skills
Free Spoken English, Artificial Intelligence (AI) literacy coaching, competitive exam prep, and quarterly job hubs.
Heritage & Youth
BoroDurga Art Village, community library, traditional handicraft workshops, and leadership mentoring for youth.

1. A message from
our President

“Rooted in the timeless soul of Bengal, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha conducts Ranaghat's grandest Durgotsav with two inseparable vows — spiritual and artistic splendor that welcomes millions, and tireless community seva that touches every neighbor's life.”

Dear devotees, members, and pilgrims across Bengal and India, it is an honor and a profound spiritual responsibility to lead Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha as President.

Over 55 years ago, visionary youth of Kamalpur came together to worship Maa Durga and uplift our locality. Today, through the devotion of our members, Krishnanagar and Kumartuli master sculptors, and the support of the Nadia administration, our Durgotsav is celebrated across the nation — culminating in 2026's historic presentation of 117 Divine Forms of Maa Durga across 80 temple pavilions.

Yet our greatest devotion will always be our seva: 300+ units of blood donated annually, free clinics near Kamalpur Primary Health Centre, youth education and skill development academies, and daily Maha Bhog for 25,000+ devotees.
