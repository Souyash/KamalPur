Who We Are

Not just a club —
a family since 1970

Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha was born in the historic town of Ranaghat, Nadia in 1970 out of a shared dream among local youth to cultivate cultural pride, sportsmanship, and selfless community welfare.

Over the last five and a half decades, our Sangha has evolved into an iconic socio-cultural institution in Bengal. Beyond creating nationally celebrated Durga Puja pandals that draw millions of visitors, we operate as our neighborhood's backbone — organizing life-saving blood donation drives, training underprivileged youth in sports, extending medical relief, and fostering unity across all walks of life.

We are not merely event organizers; we are your neighbors, your teammates, and your family in Ranaghat.

🎭
Culture & Arts
Pioneering architectural pandal arts, Rabindra-Nazrul Jayanti, drama, baul sangeet, and youth recitations.
🤝
Community Welfare
One of Nadia district's largest annual blood donation drives, free medical diagnostics, and disaster relief.
⚽
Sports & Athletics
Historic football tournaments on Sangha Prangon, grassroots coaching, cricket leagues, and annual sports days.
🌱
Youth Development
Educational stipends, community library, open drawing contests, and leadership pathways for local youth.

A message from
our President

“Rooted in the timeless soul of Bengal, Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha will forever remain committed to two inseparable pillars — fearless artistic creativity that touches millions, and humble community service that wipes the tear from every neighbor's eye.”

Dear members, devotees, and well-wishers of Ranaghat, it is an honor and a profound responsibility to serve Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha.

Over 55 years ago, a handful of visionary local youths gathered under a humble banyan tree in Kamalpur with nothing but love for football, devotion for Maa Durga, and a burning desire to help their village. Today, through the sweat of hundreds of selfless club workers, generous patrons, and the trust of the local administration, our Sangha's Durgotsav is spoken about across India and internationally.

Yet our greatest trophy will never be an award banner; it is the 300+ bottles of blood we deliver to regional hospitals, the children playing football on our grounds, and the warmth we share as one unified Kamalpur family.

Our Timeline

Five decades
of history

From humble beginnings on the soil of Kamalpur, Ranaghat, to becoming one of Bengal's most distinguished cultural icons.

1970 — The Genesis
Founded by passionate neighborhood youth on the sacred ground of Kamalpur with a grassroots football club and community prayer.
1982 — Social Welfare Wing
Launched our first annual Blood Donation Camp and established free primary medical and education assistance for the locality.
2005 — Architectural Theme Era
Pioneered thematic eco-friendly pandal concepts in Nadia district, winning top state accolades for artistry, safety, and craftsmanship.
2024–2026 — Global Recognition
Achieved national spotlight with the monumental 112-foot idol concept and the internationally celebrated 117-forms of Durga presentation.
