Who We Are

Conductors of Ranaghat's
Grandest Durgotsav Since 1970

Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha was born in 1970 in Kamalpur, Ranaghat, Nadia out of a passionate collective dream: to unite our community through the sacred celebration of Maa Durga and to serve the people through selfless humanitarian works.

Over the past 55+ years, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha has established Ranaghat on the global map of Durga Puja. Devotees and visitors travel from across West Bengal, Kolkata, and beyond to witness our pathbreaking festival creations — from the record-setting 112-foot idol to our 2026 spiritual landmark of 117 divine forms of Goddess Durga across 80 temple pavilions.

This same festival spirit fuels our year-round social welfare: conducting Nadia district's largest annual blood donation drives, running free health and eye clinics near Kamalpur Primary Health Centre, fostering grassroots youth talent in the Kamalpur Football League, and distributing daily Maha Bhog to over 25,000 pilgrims.

Culture & Arts
Pioneering architectural pandal arts, Rabindra-Nazrul Jayanti, drama, baul sangeet, and youth recitations.
Community Welfare
One of Nadia district's largest annual blood donation drives, free medical diagnostics, and disaster relief.
Sports & Athletics
Historic football tournaments on Sangha Prangon, grassroots coaching, cricket leagues, and annual sports days.
Youth Development
Educational stipends, community library, open drawing contests, and leadership pathways for local youth.

our President

“Rooted in the timeless soul of Bengal, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha conducts Ranaghat's grandest Durgotsav with two inseparable vows — spiritual and artistic splendor that welcomes millions, and tireless community seva that touches every neighbor's life.”

Dear devotees, members, and pilgrims across Bengal and India, it is an honor and a profound spiritual responsibility to lead Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha.

Over 55 years ago, visionary youth of Kamalpur came together to worship Maa Durga and uplift our locality. Today, through the devotion of our members, Krishnanagar and Kumartuli master sculptors, and the support of the Nadia administration, our Durgotsav is celebrated across the nation — culminating in 2026's historic presentation of 117 Divine Forms of Maa Durga across 80 temple pavilions.

Yet our greatest devotion will always be our seva: 300+ units of blood donated annually, free clinics near Kamalpur Primary Health Centre, youth sports championships, and daily Maha Bhog for 25,000+ devotees.

The Past Story of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha

মাটির গন্ধ, যুবকদের সংকল্প ও পাঁচ দশকের অবিস্মরণীয় সমাজসেবা

How a modest gathering of Ranaghat youth under an ancient banyan tree with a football in 1970 evolved into Nadia's most distinguished cultural institution, monumental Durga Puja epicenter, and year-round humanitarian sanctuary.

1970 — 1979 · Chapter I I

The Soil, The Tree & The Resolve

কামালপুরের মাটিতে সূচনা ও মুষ্টিভিক্ষার পূজা

In the turbulent autumn of 1970, when youth across Bengal sought direction and solidarity, a band of spirited local boys gathered on the open grounds of Kamalpur, Ranaghat. With a single football and a small bamboo hut under the banyan tree, they established Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha.

Their first Durgotsav was funded not by corporate sponsorships, but by door-to-door Musti Chanda — handfuls of unboiled rice and two-anna coins contributed by neighborhood mothers. That sacred bond between devotees and the Sangha became our unbreakable bedrock.

  • Grassroots neighborhood football league inaugurated
  • First Ekchala Pratima worshipped under a canvas tarpaulin canopy
  • 1975: Formal registration under West Bengal Societies Act
1980 — 1999 · Chapter II II

Festival Meets Human Seva

উৎসবের সাথে সমাজকল্যাণ ও রক্তদান মহাযজ্ঞ

The 1980s redefined the Sangha’s soul. When severe river floods displaced thousands in Nadia, club members converted the festival ground into an emergency relief kitchen, distributing food, blankets, and pure water for weeks.

In 1982, the club inaugurated its historic Annual Mega Voluntary Blood Donation Camp in partnership with the Indian Red Cross. Over the past 44 unbroken years, this drive has generated thousands of life-saving units, establishing our enduring motto: "Sevai Dharmo, Shongskritii Praan" (Service is Faith, Culture is Life).

  • 1982: 44-year unbroken Voluntary Blood Donation Camp launched
  • 1991: Sangha Bhavan headquarters and Netaji Free Library established
  • Emergency oxygen cylinder bank & free student coaching initiated
2000 — 2018 · Chapter III III

Pioneering Theme Architecture

নদীয়া জেলায় থিম পুজোর বৈপ্লবিক নবজাগরণ

At the turn of the millennium, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha broke the geographic boundary of Durga Puja themes, bringing world-class artistic innovation outside Kolkata right into Ranaghat.

From replicating the terracotta temples of Bishnupur to recreating the stone-carved caves of Ajanta and eco-friendly Dokra installations, the Sangha drew visitors by special suburban trains from Kolkata, Murshidabad, and North 24 Parganas, winning multiple Biswa Bangla Sharad Samman honors.

  • Pioneered eco-friendly bamboo, jute, and terracotta themes in Nadia
  • Introduced 1.5 km Chandannagar kinetic 3D LED illumination boulevards
  • Upgraded athletic ground into floodlit stadium for Kamalpur Gold Cup
2019 — 2026 · Chapter IV IV

The Golden Era & 117 Forms of Durga

১১২ ফুটের ঐতিহাসিক ভাবনা ও ১১৭ রূপের বিশ্বজোড়া খ্যাতি

The modern era has seen Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha achieve international acclaim. The monumental 112-foot idol concept and the subsequent historic presentation of 117 scriptural forms of Devi Durga across 80 sacred mini-mandirs captured national television headlines.

Over 1.2 million pilgrims visit Ranaghat during the five festive days. Yet despite global fame, the club’s heartbeat remains firmly rooted in its founding values: pristine devotion, communal harmony, eco-responsibility, and round-the-clock seva for every visitor.

  • Over 1.2 million pilgrims hosted safely with modern biometric crowd corridors
  • 25,000+ devotees served Maha Bhog Prasad daily during festival days
  • Youth Football Academy training 120+ underprivileged athletes year-round

The Master Artists Behind Our Grandeur

Revered as one of Bengal's foremost idol sculptors, Pradip Rudra Pal and his studio sculpted the internationally acclaimed 117 scriptural forms of Devi Durga and conceptualized the monumental 112-foot installation for Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha.

117 Forms of Durga, 112-Ft Clay Model, Classical Ekchala Daker Saaj & Abhay Mudra Protima.
