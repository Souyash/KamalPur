Artists & Craftsmen The Creative Soul of Ranaghat Durgotsav

The Master Artists &
Visionary Craftsmen

Durga Puja at Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha is a living canvas where master idol sculptors, thematic spatial architects, Chandannagar illumination engineers, dhak maestri, and folk muralists unite to create India's most celebrated sacred sanctuary.

Guild of Creators

Honoring the Hands That Shape Devotion

For over 56 years, our festival has stood as a beacon of artistic excellence. Every year, over 250 skilled artisans from Kumartuli, Chandannagar, Medinipur, Murshidabad, and Nadia labor for 8 continuous months to transform imagination into tangible spiritual wonder.

Nitish Munshi (শ্রী নীতিশ মুন্সী)

Lead Idol Sculptor • Official Theme: “গর্ভগুহার অন্তরালে আদিশক্তির অনন্ত রূপ”

Renowned master sculptor Nitish Munshi crafts the divine heart of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha's historic 2026 Durgotsav. Handcrafting the sanctum's divine Maa Durga Mahishasuramardini idol and leading the sculptural expression of the 117 sacred Durga forms, Sri Munshi synthesizes sacred Ganga silt, Vedic Shastric iconography, and the sacred ritual of Chokkhu Daan to awaken living spiritual shakti.

PR

Pradip Rudra Pal

Chief Clay Sculptor & Idol Architect
📍 Kumartuli, North Kolkata · 4th Gen Dynasty
2005 – 2026

Fourth-generation inheritor of the legendary Kumartuli clay sculpting dynasty, Pradip Rudra Pal has spent over 35 years sculpting divine forms for Bengal's premier pujas. At Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha, he conceptualized and hand-sculpted the monumental 117 scriptural forms of Devi Durga, marrying traditional Ganga silt clay with classical Sanskrit iconography.

Tradition Ganga Clay, Straw Binding & Classical Ekchala / Dhaaker Saaj
Accolades 7x Biswakarma Shilpa Puraskar, Asian Paints Sharad Shamman (2018, 2022)
Creations 117 Forms Sanctum, 108 Durga Avatar Darshan, Mahishasuramardini
Artistic Philosophy
"Maa Durga is not mere clay and straw. She is the living mother of Bengal. Each layer of Ganga soil carries centuries of prayer, awakening the divine shakti in every devotee who enters our mandap."
✓ Master Artisan
SR

Susanta Ray & Subrata Dey

Pandal Architects & Spatial Designers
📍 Kolkata & Ranaghat · Thematic Spatial Studio
2012 – 2026

Celebrated architectural visionaries of Bengal's contemporary Puja movement, Ray & Dey specialize in massive sustainable installations crafted purely from bamboo, terracotta tiles, Dokra brass accents, and handmade jute fabric. For our 2026 Durgotsav, they engineered the awe-inspiring 80 interconnected temple pavilions across 45,000 square feet.

Mediums Nadia Bamboo, Terracotta Relief Tiles, Dokra Brass, Organic Jute
Accolades Bengal Thematic Pandal Excellence Award (2024), Nadia Heritage Trophy
Creations 80 Temple Sanctum Complex, Terracotta Whispering Corridor, Vedic Dome
Architectural Scale
Over 65,000 seasoned bamboo poles 18,000 hand-pressed terracotta plaques Zero synthetic plastics or non-biodegradable foams
✓ Master Artisan
BP

Babu Pal & Sridam Das

Chandannagar Illumination Engineers
📍 Chandannagar, Hooghly · Lighting Guild Maestros
1994 – Present

Carrying forward Chandannagar’s legendary lighting heritage for 32 continuous years, Babu Pal and Sridam Das design the iconic 1.5-kilometer illuminated grand avenue from Ranaghat Station to the Sangha grounds. Their kinetic computerized arches depict animated mythological sequences from the Ramayana, Mahishasuramardini, and Bengal's heritage.

Engineering Kinetic 3D LED Arches, Microcontroller Storyboard Illumination
Accolades Chandannagar Light Master Guild Gold Medal (2016, 2023)
Creations 1.5 km Station Boulevard Gate, Dancing Peacock 3D Arch, Cosmic Chariot
Lighting Technology
Over 250,000 energy-efficient smart LEDs 24 dynamic moving storytelling frames Dedicated silent green generator safety grid
✓ Master Artisan
GD

Gokul Das & Motilal Troupe

Traditional Dhak & Percussion Ensemble
📍 Kandi, Murshidabad · 4 Generations of Dhak Heritage
1988 – Present

A 60-member percussion collective led by master dhaki Gokul Das, this family troupe has provided the heartbeat of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha for 38 unbroken years. Their hypnotic, resonant rhythms accompany the sacred Bodhon, the 108-lamp Sandhi Puja crescendo, the thrilling Dhunuchi Nritya, and the grand Vijaya Dashami Churni river procession.

Tradition Traditional Bengal Dhak, Kanshi, Shingha & Tasa Bol Formats
Service 38 Consecutive Years of Unbroken Ritual Leadership at Kamalpur
Key Beats Sandhi Puja 108-beat crescendo, Maha Ashtami Dhunuchi Naach Bol
Heritage Commitment
60 trained traditional percussionists Crafted from seasoned mango wood and goatskin Preserving endangered rural Bengal talas & bol rhythms
✓ Master Artisan
MC

Maniklal Chitrakar

Bengal Pata Chitra & Folk Scroll Murals
📍 Naya, Pingla, Paschim Medinipur · Patua Guild
2016 – 2026

A celebrated master of Bengal's ancient Patachitra scroll painting tradition, Maniklal Chitrakar uses exclusively hand-ground organic pigments derived from teak leaves, turmeric, crushed conch shells, and Aparajita flower petals. His massive narrative murals adorn the interior walls of our temple complex, narrating Devi Mahatmya hymns and traditional village folklore.

Medium Organic Vegetable & Mineral Dyes on Handcrafted Cloth-Paper Scrolls
Accolades West Bengal State Master Craftsperson Award (2019)
Creations Devi Mahatmya 100-foot mural gallery, Navadurga Folk Scroll cycle
Traditional Pigments
Deep Blue: Aparajita flower extract Vibrant Yellow: Raw organic turmeric & clay Lustrous White: Roasted and pulverized river conch
✓ Master Artisan
DB

Pt. Debojyoti Banerjee & Swapan Chakraborty

Sacred Soundscape & Theme Music Composers
📍 Ranaghat & Nadia · Acoustic Heritage Center
2008 – Present

Acclaimed classical musicians and acoustic soundscape designers, Pandit Debojyoti Banerjee (sitar & sarod) and Swapan Chakraborty (choral arrangement) compose the custom atmospheric score for Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha every autumn. Blending pure Raag Bhairav, Shankha (conch) calls, and Vedic Chandi stotrams, their spatial audio transports visitors into a celestial meditative realm.

Genre Classical Indian Ragas, Sanskrit Vedic Stotrams & Spatial Ambient Acoustic
Instruments Sitar, Sarod, Pakhawaj, Bamboo Flutes, Vedic Shankha Chants
Creations The 117 Devi Hymn Symphony, Dawn Bodhon Raga, Evening Arati Choir
Acoustic Architecture
Multi-zone 3D spatial acoustic sound field Zero harsh frequencies — soothing devotional ambiance Original live vocal recordings by Bengal classical vocalists
✓ Master Artisan
Legacy Foundations

The Founding Pioneers (1970 – 2004)

We bow with deep gratitude to the foundational masters who established the benchmark of classical sabeki artistry during the club's formative 35 years.

Founding Chief Sculptor 1970 – 1996

Late Haripada Pal & Manoranjan Paul

Kumartuli Master Craftsmen

Traveled annually by steam train from Kolkata to Ranaghat in the 1970s, setting up temporary clay workshops on the Sangha grounds. Sculpted our inaugural 1970 Ekchala idol and trained two generations of local Nadia clay artisans.

Historic Illumination Masters 1970 – 1993

Dhiren Karmakar & Tarun Das

Pioneers of Ranaghat Tungsten Illumination

In the pre-LED era, Karmakar and Das brought magical kerosene petromax chandeliers and hand-wound dynamo rotary circuits to Kamalpur, turning Ranaghat into a glowing nighttime festival destination across the 1970s and 1980s.

To the 80+ Unsung Bamboo Craftsmen, Clay Mixers & Rigging Laborers

Behind every award-winning pandal and magnificent murti work the tireless, often anonymous hands: the village bamboo carpenters who lash towering 70-foot scaffoldings in torrential monsoon rain, the soil haulers who sieve sacred alluvial clay from the Churni and Ganga riverbanks, the fabric drapers, and the electricians on 24-hour vigil. Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha dedicates this festival to your sacred devotion.
