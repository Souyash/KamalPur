Membership Privileges

Why belong to Kamalpur?

Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha is more than an organization — it is a lifetime bond. When you join our Sangha, you become part of a 55-year tradition that champions unity, athletic pursuit, and social upliftment.

Festival Leadership
Contribute ideas and hands-on skills to build Ranaghat's world-famous Durgotsav.
Athletic Heritage
Enjoy full access to our turf football ground, gym facilities, and badminton leagues.
Social Impact
Take part in life-saving blood donation drives and compassionate disaster relief.
