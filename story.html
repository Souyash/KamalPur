Chronicle:
The Four Epochs

The Chronicle of Kamalpur

Our history is not written in stone, but in the selfless toil of three generations of residents, artists, doctors, and volunteers who built an enduring institution of faith and humanitarian service.

Chapter I 1970 – 1975

The Banyan Tree Genesis

In the monsoon of 1970, a spirited group of neighborhood boys and village elders met beneath an ancient banyan tree on Kamalpur Road in Ranaghat. Inspired by the ideal of an inclusive, caste-free Sarbojanin Durgotsav, they went door to door collecting modest subscriptions of 50 paise, 1 rupee, and handfuls of rice from 42 local families.

Under the warm amber glow of two rented kerosene petromax lanterns, our first traditional Ekchala idol was unveiled by Kumartuli master Late Haripada Pal. Friendly monsoon football matches on the open ground cemented the young club’s bond, culminating in our formal legal registration under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961 in 1975.

Chapter II 1976 – 1990

The Humanitarian Awakening

When the devastating 1978 floods submerged large parts of Nadia, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha converted its festival committee into an emergency disaster brigade. Volunteers operated rescue boats along the Churni River, established round-the-clock community kitchens, and sheltered over 4,000 displaced villagers in local school buildings.

This humanitarian awakening sparked our permanent welfare mission. In 1982, in collaboration with the Indian Red Cross Society, we inaugurated Nadia’s first large-scale voluntary blood donation camp with 120 donors on Day 1. By 1991, through community donations, we laid the foundation of Sangha Bhavan and the Netaji Free Student Reading Room on Kamalpur Road.

Chapter III 1991 – 2010

The Thematic Architecture Revolution

As the new millennium dawned, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha spearheaded a cultural revolution in Nadia district. Rather than erecting conventional cloth pandals, the Sangha partnered with master spatial architects and folk craftsmen to create monumental thematic environments reflecting India’s architectural wonders—from terracotta temples of Bishnupur to intricate Dokra metalwork.

Simultaneously, we initiated our legendary collaboration with Chandannagar lighting masters Babu Pal and Sridam Das, creating a 1.5-kilometer kinetic illuminated boulevard from Ranaghat railway station. In 1998, we founded the floodlit Kamalpur Gold Cup Football Tournament, turning our sports grounds into an electrifying arena for regional athletes.

Chapter IV 2011 – 2026

The Golden Era of 117 Forms

The past decade propelled Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha into national and international renown. In 2024, our monumental 112-foot idol concept captured nationwide media attention. Demonstrating exemplary civic maturity, our committee worked constructively with district administration and the Calcutta High Court to establish benchmark crowd safety corridors and barrier systems.

In 2025–2026, celebrating over 56 years of unbroken devotion, we unveiled our masterpiece: 117 scriptural forms of Devi Durga sculpted by Kumartuli legend Pradip Rudra Pal, housed across 80 terracotta and bamboo temple pavilions. Over 2.5 million pilgrims journeyed to Ranaghat, cementing Kamalpur’s place in Bengal's cultural eternity.

Milestone By Milestone

56-Year Chronological Archive

Key dates that defined our club’s transformation from neighborhood youth club to international cultural beacon.

1970 — Founding Gathering Under Banyan Tree
Local Ranaghat youth and elders launch Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha with 42 families and the first Ekchala Durga Puja.
1975 — Formal Registration as Society
Registered under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961 (Registration No. S/16892).
1978 — The Great Flood Relief Operations
Volunteers deploy emergency boats along the Churni river and provide cooked food to over 4,000 marooned citizens.
1982 — Annual Voluntary Blood Donation Wing
Inaugural blood camp with the Indian Red Cross Society; begins an unbroken 38+ year annual tradition.
1991 — Inauguration of Sangha Bhavan
Two-story community building and Netaji Free Student Reading Room inaugurated on Kamalpur Road.
1994 — Chandannagar Lighting Partnership
Introduction of 1.5-km computer-synchronized 3D illumination gates from Ranaghat railway station.
1998 — Launch of Kamalpur Gold Cup
Annual floodlit knockout football championship inaugurated, bringing clubs across Bengal to Ranaghat.
2005 — Nadia Thematic Pandal Revolution
Pioneered environmental and sculptural theme pujas, bagging state-level accolades and massive tourist footfalls.
2024 — 112-Ft Architectural Concept & Court Accord
Captivated the nation with monumental design; established benchmark crowd control with the Calcutta High Court.
2026 — The Sacred 117 Forms Across 80 Temples
Celebrating 56+ glorious years with 117 individual forms of Shakti housed across 80 temple pavilions.
Roll of Honor

The Founding Visionaries & Pioneers

Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha stands tall upon the shoulders of the elders and youth who gave their time, land, and resources in 1970 to birth this sacred movement:

Founding President (1970)

Late Bimal Kanta Ghosh

Revered neighborhood teacher who presided over the banyan tree gathering and donated the initial funds.

Founding General Secretary (1970)

Late Sisir Kumar Biswas

Organized door-to-door chanda collections, registered the society in 1975, and initiated the football wing.

Pioneer Seva Organizer (1982)

Dr. Amulya Chandra Roy

Veteran physician who launched the 1982 Blood Donation Camp and provided free consultations for 30 years.

Bhavan Donor & Patron (1991)

Late Tarapada Chakraborty

Donated key land parcels on Kamalpur Road for the construction of Sangha Bhavan and Netaji Library.
