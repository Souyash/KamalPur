What We Do

We address all creative, technical, and logistical dimensions of grand festival production across West Bengal. Thanks to our deep heritage roots, we're focused on efficiency, authenticity, and grand execution, which helps us understand the visionary needs of our patrons and communicate with our massive audience.

We're focused on maximizing production value and going above and beyond to ensure all sound engineering, lighting architecture, cinematography, and crowd logistics are carried out using best-practice techniques. Bengal's festival culture is renowned for artistic grandeur, and Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha has been built upon this notion of superior craftsmanship.

Driven by cooperative and collaborative partnerships with our clients, artists, and corporate sponsors, we ensure all festival deliverables and brand associations are carried to the highest standards.

Where We Are Located

Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha has expansive festival grounds and facilities conveniently located in Ranaghat, Nadia, just 75 km from Kolkata. Our central complex spans over 3.5 acres, featuring state-of-the-art stage infrastructure, workshop bays, and dedicated media centers.

Our production partners maintain rapid-deployment hubs across Ranaghat, Taherpur, and Kolkata, ensuring seamless logistics, 24/7 technical support, and swift delivery of materials for grand cultural celebrations throughout the region.

Whether you need on-site pavilion branding, high-voltage lighting grids, or nationwide live broadcasting, our strategic geographic location provides effortless connectivity by rail (Ranaghat Junction) and National Highway (NH-12).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we customize on-ground brand activations?
Yes! Dedicated kiosk spaces, product sampling booths, and experiential interactive stalls are available on festival grounds.
Are sponsorships CSR eligible?
Yes. All healthcare drives, free clinics, youth sports development, and artisan stipends are fully compliant with CSR non-profit allocations.
What is the deadline for confirming gates?
High-visibility national highway and pandal entrance gates are allocated on a rolling first-confirmed basis before Mahalaya.
