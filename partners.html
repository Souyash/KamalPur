The Partnership Advantage

Unrivaled reach and goodwill in Bengal

Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha commands immense respect across West Bengal. As one of Nadia’s most celebrated festival organizers and active community clubs, partnering with us delivers unmatched brand credibility, engagement, and visibility.

During our flagship Sharodotsav, our grounds welcome over 1,200,000 pilgrims and visitors from across the district and state. Furthermore, our round-the-year sporting fixtures, free healthcare clinics, and mega blood donation drives connect your organization directly with community upliftment.
