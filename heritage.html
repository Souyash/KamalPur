Five Decades of History &
Living Heritage

Trace the inspiring journey of Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha from a 1970 neighborhood youth initiative into one of West Bengal’s celebrated cultural landmarks.

Archival Journey

From grassroots courage
to national acclaim

Every milestone in our club’s chronicle has been forged through the dedication of ordinary residents of Ranaghat who accomplished extraordinary feats.

1970 — The Genesis
A group of passionate local boys founded Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha under a banyan tree, initiating neighborhood football friendly matches and community worship.
1975 — Formal Society Registration
Officially registered under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961, institutionalizing our welfare constitution and electoral bylaws.
1982 — Blood Donation Seva Wing
Launched Nadia district’s first large-scale voluntary blood donation drive in partnership with the Indian Red Cross Society, collecting 120 bottles on inaugural day.
1991 — Sangha Bhavan & Netaji Library
Inaugurated our two-story headquarters building on Kamalpur Road with a free reading room for local school and college students.
2005 — Pandal Theme Architecture Movement
Pioneered environmental and sculptural theme pujas in Nadia district, earning widespread critical praise and television coverage.
2015 — Floodlit Stadium Tournaments
Installed modern floodlighting on Sangha grounds, transforming the Kamalpur Gold Cup into a marquee knockout tournament featuring clubs from across Bengal.
2024 — Nationwide Attention & Monumental Art
Captivated the nation with the awe-inspiring 112-foot idol concept and our constructive dialogue with the Calcutta High Court regarding crowd safety and civil harmony.
2026 — 117 Forms of Durga & 55+ Year Jubilee
Celebrating five and a half decades of unbroken devotion and service with the historic presentation of 117 individual forms of Devi Durga.
