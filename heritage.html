Archival Journey

From grassroots devotion
to national acclaim

Every milestone in our club’s chronicle has been forged through the dedication of ordinary residents of Ranaghat who accomplished extraordinary artistic and humanitarian feats under the banner of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha.

1970 — The Genesis
A group of passionate local boys founded Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha under a banyan tree, initiating neighborhood Durga Puja and friendly football matches.
1975 — Formal Society Registration
Officially registered under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961, institutionalizing our festival committee and welfare bylaws.
1982 — Blood Donation Seva Wing
Launched Nadia district’s first large-scale voluntary blood donation drive in partnership with the Indian Red Cross Society, collecting 120 bottles on inaugural day.
1991 — Sangha Bhavan & Netaji Library
Inaugurated our two-story headquarters building on Kamalpur Road with a free reading room for local students and a dedicated puja planning chamber.
2005 — Pandal Theme Architecture Movement
Pioneered environmental and sculptural theme pujas in Nadia district, earning state awards and drawing massive crowds from across Bengal.
2015 — Floodlit Stadium Tournaments
Installed modern floodlighting on Sangha grounds, transforming the Kamalpur Gold Cup into a marquee knockout tournament featuring clubs from across Bengal.
2024 — Nationwide Attention & Monumental Art
Captivated the nation with the monumental 112-foot idol concept and our constructive dialogue with the Calcutta High Court regarding crowd safety and civil harmony.
2026 — 117 Forms Across 80 Temples
Celebrating 55+ years of unbroken devotion with the historic presentation of 117 individual forms of Devi Durga housed across 80 temple pavilions.

The Master Artists Behind Our Grandeur

যাঁদের তুলি, মাটি ও আলোর জাদুতে জীবন্ত হয়ে ওঠে আমাদের পুজো

For over 55 illustrious years, Bengal’s most renowned clay sculptors, theme architects, lighting engineers, dhak maestros, and folk muralists have poured their devotion into Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha. Here are the revered artists behind our celebrated editions.

Chief Clay Sculptor 2005 — 2026
PR
Pradip Rudra Pal
প্রদীপ রুদ্র পাল ও পরিবার
Kumartuli Master Lineage · Kolkata / Nadia

Revered as one of Bengal's foremost idol sculptors, Pradip Rudra Pal sculpted the internationally acclaimed 117 scriptural forms of Devi Durga and conceptualized the monumental 112-foot installation for Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha.

Masterpiece Creations at Kamalpur:
117 Forms of Durga, 112-Ft Clay Model, Classical Ekchala Daker Saaj & Abhay Mudra Protima.
🏆 3x Biswa Bangla Sharad Samman (Best Traditional / Thematic Idol)
Pandal Architect & Designer 2012 — 2026
SR
Susanta Ray & Subrata Dey
সুশান্ত রায় ও সুব্রত দে
Bengal Eco-Architecture & Spatial Design

Architectural visionaries who transformed Ranaghat into a living temple city. Master architects behind our 80-Temple Sanctum Replica, hand-carved terracotta facades, and sustainable bamboo filigree structures.

Masterpiece Creations at Kamalpur:
80-Temple Replica Complex, Terracotta Bishnupur Mandap, Organic Jute & Bamboo Sanctum.
🏆 Nadia Zilla Parishad Gold Trophy (Best Thematic Architecture)
Illumination & Light Engineer 1994 — Present
BP
Babu Pal & Sridam Das
বাবু পাল ও শ্রীদাম দাস
Chandannagar Illumination Guild · Hooghly

Masters of the legendary Chandannagar lighting lineage with 32 unbroken years of illuminating Ranaghat. Designers of our 1.5-km animated LED boulevard and dynamic 3D mythological storytelling chariots.

Masterpiece Creations at Kamalpur:
3D Mahishasuramardini Kinetic LED Gate, Station Road Light Arches, Solar-Hybrid Illumina.
🏆 Bengal Heritage Lighting Honor & All-Bengal Illumination Cup
Dhak & Percussion Ensemble 1988 — Present
GD
Gokul Das & Motilal Troupe
গোকুল দাস ও মতিলাল বাদক দল
Traditional Folk Dhaki Guild · Murshidabad / Nadia

A 60-member traditional percussion family that has led the spiritual heartbeat of our Durgotsav for 38 continuous years. Masters of the sacred Sandhi Puja Bol, thunderous Kanshi accompaniment, and high-energy Dhunuchi Naach pacing.

Masterpiece Creations at Kamalpur:
Maha Aarati 60-Dhak Symphony, Classical Tala Jugalbandi, Vijaya Dashami Shobhayatra Beats.
🏆 State Folk Cultural Directorate Lifetime Percussion Honor
Folk Mural & Pata Chitra 2016 — 2026
MC
Maniklal Chitrakar
মানিকলাল চিত্রকর
Pingla Pata Chitra Heritage · West Medinipur

National award-winning scroll painter whose intricate narrative murals adorn the inner walls of our pandal. Maniklal uses exclusively natural forest pigments and crushed river stones to depict Chandi Mangal folklore.

Masterpiece Creations at Kamalpur:
4,000 Sq.Ft. Chandi Mangal Natural Pigment Scroll Walls & Terracotta Clay Relief Panels.
🏆 Rashtrapati Puraskar Nominee & Bengal Folk Guild Fellowship
Devotional Soundscape Composer 2008 — Present
DB
Pt. Debojyoti Banerjee
পণ্ডিত দেবজ্যোতি ব্যানার্জী ও দল
Classical Sanskrit Stotra & Acoustic Soundscapes

Acoustic wizard who crafts the immersive auditory sanctum inside the pandal, combining ancient Vedic chants, live bamboo flutes, and shehnai strains.

Masterpiece Creations at Kamalpur:
Original "Aigiri Nandini" Orchestral Soundscape & Spatial Vedic Chant Resonance.
🏆 Bengal Performing Arts Excellence in Sacred Acoustics

Homage to Our Unsung Craftsmen & Shramik Devotees

Behind every towering pandal and glowing arch stands the tireless labor of over 200 bamboo craftsmen from Nadia, clay haulers from the banks of the sacred Ganga, lighting linemen from Chandannagar, and local volunteer families who dedicate four months before Puja. We bow to their sacred devotion.
