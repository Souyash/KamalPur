Heritage 55+ Years of Legacy · Est. 1970

Five Decades of Devotion &
Living Heritage

Trace the inspiring journey of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha — how a 1970 neighborhood Durga Puja and youth club evolved into Ranaghat's world-famous Durgotsav and humanitarian pillar.

Archival Journey

From grassroots devotion
to national acclaim

Every milestone in our club’s chronicle has been forged through the dedication of ordinary residents of Ranaghat who accomplished extraordinary artistic and humanitarian feats under the banner of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha.

1970 — The Genesis
A group of passionate local boys founded Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha under a banyan tree, initiating neighborhood Durga Puja and friendly football matches.
1975 — Formal Society Registration
Officially registered under the West Bengal Societies Registration Act, 1961, institutionalizing our festival committee and welfare bylaws.
1982 — Blood Donation Seva Wing
Launched Nadia district’s first large-scale voluntary blood donation drive in partnership with the Indian Red Cross Society, collecting 120 bottles on inaugural day.
1991 — Sangha Bhavan & Netaji Library
Inaugurated our two-story headquarters building on Kamalpur Road with a free reading room for local students and a dedicated puja planning chamber.
2005 — Pandal Theme Architecture Movement
Pioneered environmental and sculptural theme pujas in Nadia district, earning state awards and drawing massive crowds from across Bengal.
2015 — Floodlit Stadium Tournaments
Installed modern floodlighting on Sangha grounds, transforming the Kamalpur Gold Cup into a marquee knockout tournament featuring clubs from across Bengal.
2024 — Nationwide Attention & Monumental Art
Captivated the nation with the monumental 112-foot idol concept and our constructive dialogue with the Calcutta High Court regarding crowd safety and civil harmony.
2026 — 117 Forms Across 80 Temples
Celebrating 55+ years of unbroken devotion with the historic presentation of 117 individual forms of Devi Durga housed across 80 temple pavilions.
