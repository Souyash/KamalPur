About Us Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha · Ranaghat

Conductors of Ranaghat's
Grandest Durgotsav Since 1970

Rooted in the historic soil of Ranaghat, Nadia, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha (কামালপুর অভিযান সংঘ) is celebrated throughout Bengal for conducting record-breaking Durga Pujas and serving humanity through medical seva, blood drives, and youth sports.

Our Story

Devotion to Maa Durga,
dedication to community

In 1970, when a group of passionate youths in Kamalpur gathered under a local banyan tree with an old leather football, they shared a vision that extended far beyond a playground: to organize community worship of Goddess Durga and to establish an unbreakable bond of mutual aid in Ranaghat.

Over the past 55+ years, Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha has grown into an iconic socio-cultural institution in Nadia. Today, the Sangha conducts Ranaghat's crowning festive pilgrimage — famous across India for sensational artistic installations including the 112-foot idol and the 2026 presentation of 117 divine forms of Goddess Durga housed in 80 temple pavilions.

Yet the core purpose of Kamalpur Abhijan Sangha has never wavered: every festive effort directly powers our round-the-year grassroots service — voluntary blood donation drives, free clinics near Kamalpur Primary Health Centre, educational assistance, and grassroots sports for local children.

Our Mission
To conduct Bengal's most spiritually enriching and artistically sublime Durgotsav while relentlessly serving the needy through healthcare, education, and youth sports.
Our Vision
To foster an inclusive, compassionate Ranaghat where devotion to Maa Durga inspires active fraternity, self-reliance, and care for every elder and child.
Four Cornerstones

How we serve Ranaghat

Culture & Arts
Grand thematic Durgotsav, Rabindra-Nazrul Jayanti, theater productions (natok), and musical conferences honoring classical and baul traditions.
Community Welfare
Nadia’s leading voluntary blood donation drives, emergency ambulance assistance, disaster relief, and distribution of winter clothes and food.
Sports & Athletics
Kamalpur Gold Cup Football League, grassroots football academy for local kids, open badminton championships, and annual athletic meets.
Youth Development
Free reading room and community library, academic stipends for meritorious underprivileged students, and open drawing and debate contests.
Club Infrastructure

Our Facilities & Campus

Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha owns and maintains dedicated facilities accessible to members, athletes, and local residents.

Sangha Bhavan

A two-story headquarters housing the executive council room, public reading hall, and festival committee desk.

Athletic Stadium & Grounds

Full-size natural turf ground for football, athletics, and the grand festive pandal installation during Sharodotsav.

Seva Medical Unit

Weekly doctor consultation dispensary, emergency oxygen cylinders, and diagnostic checkup infrastructure.

Netaji Youth Library

Collection of over 3,000 books, competitive exam study materials, and daily Bengali and English newspapers.
