Not Just a Club —
A Family Since 1970

Rooted in the soil of Ranaghat, Nadia, Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha is a registered socio-cultural non-profit dedicated to Bengal's artistic heritage, grassroots sportsmanship, and selfless humanitarian seva.

Our Story

Born out of passion,
sustained by community

In 1970, when a group of passionate youngsters in Kamalpur gathered under a local banyan tree with an old leather football, they shared a vision that extended far beyond a playground. They dreamt of creating an organization where cultural preservation and community welfare were inseparable.

Over the past 55+ years, Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha has grown from a humble neighborhood club into one of the most prominent cultural institutions in Nadia district. While our Durga Puja installations now make national headlines, our heartbeat remains our year-round grassroots service: voluntary blood donation drives, free health clinics, financial aid for students, and sports development for underprivileged youth.

🎯
Our Mission
To preserve and elevate Bengal’s classical, folk, and festival arts while relentlessly serving the needy through healthcare, education, and youth sports.
🌟
Our Vision
To foster an inclusive, empathetic community in Ranaghat where tradition nurtures progress, and no neighbor is left without assistance in times of distress.
Four Cornerstones

How we serve Ranaghat

🎭
Culture & Arts
Grand thematic Durgotsav, Rabindra-Nazrul Jayanti, theater productions (natok), and musical conferences honoring classical and baul traditions.
🤝
Community Welfare
Nadia’s leading voluntary blood donation drives, emergency ambulance assistance, disaster relief, and distribution of winter clothes and food.
⚽
Sports & Athletics
Kamalpur Gold Cup Football League, grassroots football academy for local kids, open badminton championships, and annual athletic meets.
🌱
Youth Development
Free reading room and community library, academic stipends for meritorious underprivileged students, and open drawing and debate contests.
Club Infrastructure

Our Facilities & Campus

Kamalpur Abhijaan Sangha owns and maintains dedicated facilities accessible to members, athletes, and local residents.

🏛️

Sangha Bhavan

A two-story headquarters housing the executive council room, public reading hall, and festival committee desk.

⚽

Athletic Stadium & Grounds

Full-size natural turf ground for football, athletics, and the grand festive pandal installation during Sharodotsav.

🩺

Seva Medical Unit

Weekly doctor consultation dispensary, emergency oxygen cylinders, and diagnostic checkup infrastructure.

📚

Netaji Youth Library

Collection of over 3,000 books, competitive exam study materials, and daily Bengali and English newspapers.
